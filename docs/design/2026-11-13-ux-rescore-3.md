# UX re-score 3 (2026-11-13)

Reviewer: independent design review (Claude, read-only; no source changed, no device contacted).
Rubric: the nine lenses (a to i, 0 to 5 each), eight checks and arithmetic of `2026-11-12-ux-rescore.md` and `2026-11-12-ux-rescore-2.md`. Score = lens sum / 45, scaled to 100.
Only pixels were judged. No commit message, changelog or agent report was read to learn what should have changed.

The HUD in them no longer matches the gallery: status text is 31 px cap on the device and 39 px in the gallery, and the card names "MAROON" with no number while the gallery names "9 YELLOW STRIPE". So lens c and checks 3 to 5 describe an overlay that may already have changed. Nothing here credits or penalises an overlay change that no still shows.

Method.
- **Pixel sampling.** A small CoreGraphics tool (temporary, not committed) gave the mean colour of a box, the ink bounding box above or below a luminance threshold, and the rows that contain ink. Cap height was measured on single capital letters or digits, with no descenders, at a 50 % threshold, so it is about plus or minus 1 px. Contrast is the WCAG ratio between the most extreme glyph pixel and a sampled plate pixel. That gives the glyph core, so anti-aliased edges read a little lower.
  - Sampled stills: `ipad-tier-easy`, `ipad-tier-hard`, `ipad-tier-hard-dark`, `ipad-shot-made`, `ipad-shot-missed`, `ipad-shot-scratch`, `ipad-called-off-line`, `ipad-called-off-line-aimed`, `ipad-called-no-pocket`, `ipad-called-on-line`, `ipad-blocked-shot`, `ipad-guide-draw`, `ipad-no-clean-shot`, `ipad-ball-unknown`, `ipad-banner-tracking-lost`, `ipad-losing-balls`, `ipad-sheet-settings`, `se-tier-easy`, and the three device stills.
- **Text recognition.** Tesseract OCR was run on all 182 gallery stills. It does not read green text on a dark plate, so "Table locked: 9 ft" was found by eye, not by OCR.
- **Viewed by eye.** About 20 stills were opened at full size, plus one 2x crop of the overlay in `build-e898b55`.
- **Physical sizes.** 1 px = 0.096 mm on the device glass. The visual angle at 2 m is size / 2000 mm.
- **Estimates.** Anything marked "estimated" was judged by eye or from geometry, not measured.

## 4. New problems neither earlier review listed

Ranked by how much each would bother a player at the table.

1. **A called shot can show two pockets on one card.** `ipad-called-off-line`: the headline says "4 PURPLE -> FAR-RIGHT CORNER", the CALLED line says "FAR-LEFT CORNER", the subtitle says "Off line" with no direction, and the Stun tile belongs to the far-right shot the player did not call. The player has to work out which pocket the card is about. When a pocket is called, the card should be about that pocket only.
2. **The called-shot card is tall enough to sit on the table (estimated for the device).** In called play the card has four rows (tier, headline, "Off line", CALLED) and runs from y = 206 to about 610 px (`ipad-called-off-line-aimed`). On the device framing (`build-e898b55`) the card starts at about y = 195 and the far rail is at about y = 583. A card this tall would cover the far rail and both far corner pockets, which is where "FAR-RIGHT CORNER" is. Shot states add a score chip below the card, reaching about y = 560 (`ipad-shot-missed`). Not measured on a device because none of this HUD has been captured there.
3. **The card jumps 9 px when the aim crosses the line.** Measured: card top y = 215 px off line, 206 px on line, because the capsule icon changes height (§2, check 6). The jump happens exactly while the player is fine-tuning the stick, and it reads as a flicker.
4. **"Device on a stand" is off in the gallery.** `ipad-sheet-settings` shows the toggle off. The sheet's own footnote says that held in the hand the app "aims where the camera points". On a tripod that is the wrong behaviour. Whether off is the shipped default cannot be told from a still. If it is, every tripod user starts in the wrong mode.
5. **The result is said twice.** `ipad-shot-streak`: "+1 · 3 IN A ROW" in the card and "3 of 3 made · 3 in a row" in the chip below it. `ipad-shot-made`: "+1" and "1 of 1 made". `ipad-called-on-line`: "On line — send it" in the capsule and "On line" in the card. Each repeat costs a row near the table.
6. **Called-shot play hides tracking health.** In `ipad-called-off-line`, `-off-line-aimed` and `-on-line` the capsule carries the aiming message instead of "Tracking N balls". A drop in ball count while aiming would show nothing until it becomes a loss. The no-pocket state keeps the count (`ipad-called-no-pocket`).
7. **iPhone SE, largest text: the card hides the shot.** `se-called-off-line-aimed-maxtext`: the card covers the far two-thirds of the table drawing, and the 4 ball it names is not visible. Low rank because the iPad on a tripod is the target, but the SE is in the gallery.
8. **The Stun tile appears and disappears for the same shot.** It shows in `ipad-called-no-pocket`, `-off-line` and `-on-line`, but not in `-off-line-aimed`. All four show "4 PURPLE -> FAR-RIGHT CORNER" with the same ball positions. The tile vanishing is one more thing that moves while aiming.
9. **Two names for one setting.** Bar "Any group" (every tracking still); Settings "Ball group: Open" (`ipad-sheet-settings`).
10. **Evidence labelling.** INDEX.md lists `ipad-losing-balls-dark` and `se-losing-balls-dark` with variant "light, default text". The still is the dark appearance. Its description says "more light would help", but the capsule says only "Only seeing 6 of 15 balls", with no light advice. The scene, its label or the capsule copy is wrong.

## 5. Is 70 reached?

**Yes, at the line: 70.0 (31.5 / 45), plus or minus 6, on Simulator evidence for the HUD and one-build-old device evidence for the overlay.** I would not report it as "70 confirmed". Two things put it at risk:
- My own reading of the overlay still is half a point lower than review 2's: 3 against 3.5 on lens c, which alone gives 68.9.
- No device still shows the HUD now in the gallery.

The first item below decides whether 70 holds.

### What separates this from 80 (36 / 45, +4.5 lens points)

Most valuable first. Each item names the lens it moves.

1. **Capture the current build on the device**, one dim and one bright room. Three stills: tracking with a shot, a called shot off line, a make. This is the gate for every other item: it either confirms 70 or exposes the gap, and it unlocks checks 1, 4, 5 and 6 and lenses c and h. (No lens points by itself; removes most of the plus or minus 6.)
2. **Make the object leg the strongest mark, and put the ring on the mouth.**
   - Object leg at least 14 px in a light green near #5BE99F. By the sampled cloth luminance that is about 7 to 8 : 1, against 2.9 : 1 now.
   - Cue leg down to 10 px white at 70 %.
   - Pocket ring in its own hue (white, 3 px), centred on the pocket-mouth centre at 1.5 x the mouth width, with the line ending at that centre.
   - Verify on a device still with the mouth marked.
   - c: 3.5 to 4.5 (+1); g +0.25.
3. **One pocket per called card.** When a pocket is called, the headline becomes the called shot ("4 PURPLE -> FAR-LEFT CORNER") with "CALLED" as a small tag in the tier row. Drop the CALLED line, and give the tile and the tier for that pocket. "Off line" always carries a direction in player terms: "Off line — aim a little thinner" or "a little fuller", not "right" or "left". i +0.5, e +0.25, b +0.25.
4. **Cap the card at three rows and stop it moving.**
   - Merge the score into the card: "MADE IT · +1 · 3 IN A ROW · 3 of 3". No chip below.
   - Reserve a fixed-height icon box in the capsule so the card top never moves. That closes check 6.
   - Keep the card bottom at least 60 px above the far rail on the device framing.
   - h +0.5, b +0.25.
5. **Payoff on the table, not only on the plate.** On a make, a ring burst at the pocket that was made, 0.6 s. On a miss, keep the ghost and the line for 2 s at 60 %. d 3 to 4 (+1); needs a motion review, not stills.
6. **The AR half of setup in stills, in player words.**
   - Capture the corner handles, Lock and "Use pockets" on the device.
   - Change "Tap the cushion-nose corners" to "Tap the four corners where the cushions meet".
   - a 3 to 3.5 (+0.5).
7. **Say the true reason a pick is blocked.** "2 BLUE is in the way", or "No angle to any pocket", from the same check that blocked it. i +0.25.
8. **Sheets.** Picker values to #6C6C70 or darker (about 4.6 : 1 or better on white), "Ball group: Any group", and start "Device on a stand" on (or ask once at first launch). f 3.5 to 4 (+0.5).

Estimated: items 2 to 8 add about +5 lens points, which gives 36.5 / 45 = 81. Items 3, 4 and 7 are correctness and layout fixes with no new design and are the cheapest points. Item 2 is the largest single gain and needs the device.

## 6. Simply wrong

- **`ipad-blocked-shot`: the reason does not match the table (geometry estimated from the placeholder).** The card says "No angle — that cut is past 90°". The cue ball (about x 385, y 750 in the displayed still), the 2 ball (657, 750) and the 4 ball (1135, 715) are nearly collinear: the cue-to-4 line passes within about 13 display px of the 2 ball's centre, inside its radius. The 4 is blocked by the 2. From that cue position the cut into either right corner is roughly 25 to 35 degrees, not past 90.
- **`ipad-called-off-line`: two pockets on one card.** Headline "-> FAR-RIGHT CORNER", CALLED line "FAR-LEFT CORNER", and a Stun tile for the uncalled shot (§4 item 1).
- **`ipad-called-on-line`: the same statement twice.** "On line — send it" (capsule) and "On line" (card).
- **INDEX.md `*-losing-balls-dark` rows say variant "light"** and describe light advice that the still does not show (§4 item 10).
- **`ipad-launch` and `ipad-calibrate-*`: "Run on an iPhone to see the table." on an iPad.** Simulator-only placeholder; listed for completeness, as in review 2.
- No text clips or overlaps in any still opened or in the OCR output.

## 7. What I could not judge

- **Any overlay on the current build.** Lens c and checks 3 to 5 rest on `build-e898b55` and `build-20e742d`, which are one or more builds old.
- **The current HUD on a real device.** Type size, card height against the far rail, and contrast over a real camera image.
