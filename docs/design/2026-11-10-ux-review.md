# CueSync AR: independent design and UX review (2026-11-10)

Reviewer: independent design/UX pass (Claude, read-only on `App/` and `Packages/`).
Code reviewed: `origin/claude/ipad-remote-run` @ `c7ee941` (simulator builds were made from it).
Real device: iPad 9th gen, build `1a008d5*`, parked at the table, calibrated, tracking 4 balls.
Question: **is this a polished, arcade-style assisting app, and what exactly stops it from being one?**

Images are in [`review-images/`](review-images/). They show app screens only. The two real-iPad
images are tight crops of the HUD cards; the room is cropped out. No room photo or footage is
committed.

---

## 1. Verdict

**No. Polish score: 34 / 100.**

CueSync is an honest, well-engineered **instrument**. It is not yet an arcade-style **assistant**.
The September critique ("a control panel") is half fixed. The bottom bar went from nine controls
to four or five, all labelled, and the top went from eight stacked capsules to one status line
plus one toast. The degraded-tracking copy is now reachable, and "Device is parked" is in
Settings. Those were real wins, and I verified them in code and on screen.

What still separates it from "arcade" falls into four groups:

1. **No feedback for anything.** The whole app has one haptic, `.sensoryFeedback(.success)` on
   calibration lock (`CalibrationOverlayView.swift:93`). It probably never fires, because the
   same lock call unmounts that view (`SessionModel+Calibration.swift:123`; needs a device check).
   There are no sounds. Voice guidance exists but defaults to off (`SettingsModel.swift:202`).
   Nothing animates on the table: the renderer deletes and rebuilds every entity on each change
   (`ARSessionCoordinator.swift:736`), so rings, lines and ghost balls pop rather than move. The
   app never notices a shot being made, so it cannot celebrate one.
2. **The table overlay is not the hero.** It is hairline-thin: 8 mm strips (`:692`), and the
   recommended plan is drawn at 4 mm and 40 % opacity (`:763`, `:772`). The live device-pose aim
   line is the brightest thing on screen even when nobody is aiming. On the real parked iPad, an
   amber two-cushion bank from the cue ball dominated the table while the suggested shot was a
   faint green dash. After drift, every ring sat about one ball-width off its ball.
3. **It says confident things it does not know.** The real iPad showed **"100 %"** for a 24.6°
   cut into a side pocket, called a dark-blue ball the 8-ball at colour confidence 0.9, and rated
   all three offered shots "easy" (100 / 99 / 78 %). The card says "Best shot" over a coral 38 %
   "hard" shot. The 05-UX-DESIGN "confidence honesty" rule (overlays fade to 40 % when tracking
   degrades) is **not implemented**: `HUDStatus.overlayOpacity` is defined
   (`HUDComponents.swift:102`) and never read anywhere.
4. **First run is an engineer's flow.** There is no welcome screen. The camera permission prompt
   fires over a black screen. The capsule says "Point at the table" while the real way in is
   "tap anywhere". Calibration asks for "cushion-nose corners". Failure toasts like "Fit is 8 cm
   out. cornerTopLeft disagrees with the others by 10 cm" use raw identifiers and vanish after
   2.5 s (`SessionModel.swift:425`). Settings opens on "Detector / Roboflow API key /
   Secrets.xcconfig / Neural Engine" before a player reaches anything they care about.

The bones are good: one status line, labelled controls, a shot card with a big number, pocket
discs, and a ghost ball. What is missing is everything that makes those bones feel alive and
trustworthy. A focused two-week arcade pass (section 6) could plausibly move the score into the
60s. That pass would cover juice, line weight, honest numbers and the calibration copy. No new
perception work is needed.

## 4. Ranked fix list (top 10 by value for effort)

| # | Change | Effort | Impact | Concrete proposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Make the recommended shot the hero line; suppress the live aim line when parked or not aiming** | 4-6 h | Very high | When `deviceParked`, or when the aim pose has been still for more than 1.5 s with no stick, draw no live strip. Raise the plan strips to the live weight (`stripWidth` about 14-16 mm, opacity 0.9) with a 2 px darker under-stroke for bright cloth. Make the ghost ball an outline ring plus a 0.5-opacity white fill, not a 25 % green sphere. |
| 2 | **Name the ball, and stop claiming 100 %** | 3-4 h | Very high | Card headline: "**Blue → right side**" (ball colour or number, then the pocket in *viewer* terms: left/right/near/far from camera pose). Replace raw % with a band word in large type: "EASY / MAKEABLE / HARD", plus a small "~80 %" capped at 95 %. Do not label a shot below "makeable" as "Best shot"; say "Best available". |
| 3 | **Apply `overlayOpacity`** (05-UX "confidence honesty") | 1-2 h | High | Pass `hudStatus.overlayOpacity` into `OverlayRenderer` as a material multiplier. Also fade to 0.4 when `anchorDriftMm` or the pocket-reprojection error exceeds about 30 mm. |
| 4 | **Juice pack v1: haptics, sound and motion for four moments** | 6-8 h | High | Lock: `.success` haptic fired from RootView (not the unmounting overlay), table-outline sweep 400 ms, chime. Ball found: ring scales 0.6 → 1.0 with a 40 ms stagger. Shot chosen: target ring pulse twice plus a light tick. On-line: the existing `onLine` status adds a rigid haptic and pocket-ring flash. Respect Reduce Motion and a "Sounds" toggle. |
| 5 | **Rewrite calibration copy; make refusals point at the table** | 4-6 h | High | Replace "cushion-nose corners" with "Tap the 4 corner pockets" (pocket path as default). Pocket chips use viewer terms ("Near left", "Far right"). A refusal names the pocket in words and pulses that pocket's ring red: "The far-left pocket doesn't fit — tap it again." Never print `y=`, `cornerTopLeft`, "pass h". Calibration errors persist until the next action instead of a 2.5 s timeout. |
| 6 | **Empty states: no shot and break** | 3-4 h | Medium-high | No legal pot: card "No clean pot — play safe" in amber with the nearest safe target ring. Full rack: "Break — aim at the head ball" with a fixed guide line. |
| 7 | **Lock moment and remembered table** | 2 h | Medium-high | Replace "Locked 2.34 × 1.17 m — 8 ft table" with "You're set — table remembered." Put the size in a tappable chip ("8 ft? Change"), because the pockets cannot see the size. |
| 8 | **Hide developer surfaces from players** | 2-3 h | Medium | Gate Settings → Detection and Detector compute, the More → Debug mirror, Address and build rows, and the build badge behind `DeveloperMode.isUnlocked`. In a release build with developer mode locked, More becomes "Record session · Settings · Help". Delete the refactor-history footers. |
| 9 | **Contrast pass** | 2-3 h | Medium | Bar and card text: white on a darker scrim (`.regularMaterial` plus 30 % black) with the green as an icon or underline only. A lighter felt green (`#4CD38E`) for text, about 4.7 : 1 on the measured bar background (to verify). Fill the card stroke with the band colour and add the band word so difficulty is not colour-only. |
| 10 | **Welcome plus stance card (S1/S2 from the September proposal)** | 6-8 h | Medium | One welcome screen with the one-sentence promise and "Get started" (which triggers the camera prompt). A one-card stance coach that auto-dismisses when the plane is found. A camera-denied full-screen state with an "Open Settings" button. |

Also worth doing, below the top ten: scale the HUD on iPad (`horizontalSizeClass == .regular`
gives a card at 1.6× size, readable at 2 m); viewer-relative pocket names everywhere in speech;
default voice to "key moments" when parked.

## 6. Minimal "arcade pass": ship first

Goal: move the score from about 34 to about 60 with roughly 3-4 days of UI-only work, and no
perception or `CueSyncCore` change.

**Day 1, the line is the hero (fix #1, #3).**
- Hide the live aim strip when parked or still; draw the recommended plan at full weight.
- Outline ghost ball; apply `overlayOpacity`.
- Acceptance: on the parked iPad, the one bright line on the table runs cue ball → ghost → pocket.

**Day 2, the card tells the truth (fix #2, #6, #7).**
- Ball name plus viewer-relative pocket; band word first, capped percentage second.
- "Best available" below makeable; no-shot and break cards.
- "You're set — table remembered" with a size chip.

**Day 3, juice (fix #4).**
- A small `HUDFeedback` helper owned by RootView. It fires haptic plus sound plus a 250-400 ms
  spring on four events: lock, balls found, shot chosen, on line.
- Ring stagger-in, target pulse.
- Reduce Motion and Sounds toggles.
- Add a single "Sounds" row in Settings → Guides.

**Day 4, cleanup (fix #5 copy only, #8, #9).**
- Remove raw IDs from calibration strings and give calibration errors persistence.
- Gate developer surfaces.
- Contrast pass on bar and card.

Deliberately **not** in the first pass: the welcome screen (fix #10), iPad HUD scaling, shot-made
detection. Shot-made detection is the single biggest remaining arcade feature, but it needs
pipeline work (pocketed-ball events) and a device run, so it is the headline of pass two.

## 7. Persona reads

**O, art director (brand fit, type, colour, dark mode).**
- The glass capsule and bar are tidy and on-system.
- The palette is four tokens doing ten jobs. Felt green is the brand colour, the "easy" colour,
  the object-path colour, the calibration colour *and* the text colour of every bar label.
  Nothing reads as primary.
- Felt-green text on glass is muddy on a light rail (img 09) and washed out in light mode
  (img 03).
- The only large type is the 30 pt percentage, which is the number least worth trusting.
- The orange monospaced build badge is the most saturated UI element on a player's screen.
- Dark mode is the only mode that works; light mode should simply not tint text green.

**K, accessibility.**
- Card and bar labels fail WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Measured from rendered pixels: 1.6-2.0 : 1 on
  the real iPad card, 3.2 : 1 on the bar, about 2.5 : 1 in light mode.
- Difficulty is encoded by colour alone on the card.
- The percentage is a fixed 30 pt; the HUD caps Dynamic Type at xxxLarge (it holds up there,
  img 07).
- Corner handles are announced as "Corner 1 handle" with no position.
- No Reduce Motion or Reduce Transparency handling anywhere.
- The 44 pt bar minimum and the combined VoiceOver label on the card ("Suggested shot: 38 per cent
  into the top-right corner, hard") are good.

**I, the real player, 10 seconds at the table.**
- "100 %, bottom side. Which ball? Which side is bottom?"
- The big orange line goes off the cushion twice and I am not even holding the cue.
- The rings are next to the balls, not on them.
- I'd trust the white disc on the cue ball and nothing else.
- After I pot it, nothing happens.

**E, operator (someone who must set it up).**
- Setup today requires knowing what a cushion nose is, or reading pocket names in table
  coordinates.
- Failure reads like "cloth height (y=-0.533)" and disappears before I finish reading it.
- The successful iPad lock needed `Scripts/calibrate-pockets.py` to search pocket coordinates.
  There is no way a venue staffer reproduces that.
- The diamond verification overlay is the best idea in the app and needs one sentence of
  coaching, not four capsules.
- Once locked, relocalization is excellent, and the app should say so out loud.
