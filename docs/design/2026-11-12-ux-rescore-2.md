The Simulator draws a flat placeholder table, not a camera and not the AR overlay, so overlay and over-scene contrast are judged only on the device stills. The two newest device stills show the same card and bar design as the gallery (layout, type sizes and copy pattern match to the pixel), so the "one build older" gap does not change any finding below except where noted.

Method. Pixel sizes and colours were measured with a small CoreGraphics script (crop, mean colour of a 5 x 5 or 13 x 13 box, ink extent above a luminance threshold). Text height is the cap height (capital letters, no descenders) at a 50 % luminance threshold, so it carries about plus or minus 1 px of anti-aliasing. Contrast ratios are WCAG relative-luminance ratios between a sampled glyph pixel and a sampled background pixel. All 160 gallery stills were run through OCR (tesseract) for the copy and developer-string checks. Physical sizes: 1 px = 0.096 mm on the 264 ppi glass; visual angle at 2 m = size / 2000 mm. Anything marked "estimated" was judged by eye or from perspective, not measured.

Baseline arithmetic note: the baseline's own lens scores sum to 21.5 / 45 = 47.8 / 100, but it printed 46. I report my score the same way (sum / 45), so the honest comparison is 48 to 63.

## 4. New problems the baseline did not list

Ranked by how much each would bother a player at the table.

1. **"Lost the table" while the HUD says all is well.** `ipad-banner-tracking-lost`: green tick, "Tracking 5 balls", an EASY shot card and a salmon "Lost the table" pill, all at once. The player sees a recommended shot from a table the app says it lost.
2. **"On line" when the shot is off line.** `ipad-called-off-line` shows a green "On line" subtitle in the card; the capsule in the same still says "Pocket called - line up the shot". `ipad-called-no-pocket` also shows "On line" before any pocket is called. In called-shot play this is the one signal the player waits for.
3. **The card can name the wrong ball.** `build-20e742d`: "ORANGE -> LEFT SIDE", but the green leg starts at the maroon 7 and the ghost disc sits beside it. A player who looks for orange finds two orange balls elsewhere on the table. Colour alone is also ambiguous on a full rack: two maroon balls (7 and 15) sit on this table.
4. **The most important line is the faintest.** Object leg 11 px at 3.4 to 3.7 : 1; cue leg 14 px at 9.9 : 1 (`build-e898b55`). From 2 m the eye lands on the grey leg, not on the path to the pocket.
5. **A make looks like a ball count.** "Made it", "Scratch", "3 in a row" are 31 px pills, the same size and shape as status (`ipad-shot-made`, `ipad-shot-streak`). There is nothing to celebrate at 2 m.
6. **Stacked pills crowd the top and will reach the table.** `ipad-shot-streak` stacks four elements; `ipad-shot-scratch` stacks three pills. On the device the card already ends about 420 px down and the far rail is about 590 px down, so a second pill under the card would sit on the far rail and pockets (estimated from `build-e898b55`). `ipad-shot-streak` also repeats the ball count ("Now tracking: 2 balls" under "Tracking 2 balls").
7. **HARD looks like an error.** HARD and BLOCKED share the same salmon chip and red card border (`ipad-tier-hard`, `ipad-blocked-shot`), and the guide subtitle "Aim right" is also salmon (`ipad-guide-draw`). A playable hard shot reads as a fault.
8. **Blocked card does not name the ball.** `ipad-blocked-shot`: "No angle - that cut is past 90 degrees" and "Tap the ball again to release", with no ball named. "Release" reads as a stroke, not "clear my pick".
9. **"Aim right" is ambiguous.** Right of what: the ball centre, the ghost, the stick? (`ipad-guide-draw`.)
10. **Table moved: the fix is not a button.** `ipad-table-moved` says "Table moved? Re-calibrate" but the bar shows only Record and More; on launch the bar carries "Set up table". Whether the capsule is tappable cannot be told from a still.
11. **Ball counts use two denominators.** A full rack reads "Tracking 16 balls" (`ipad-break-rack`, device `build-e898b55`: 15 object balls plus the cue ball), while the loss state reads "Only seeing 1 of 15 balls" with only the cue ball on the table (`ipad-losing-one-of-fifteen`). Both cannot use the same rule.
12. **Settings and sheet footnotes.** Jargon (lens f) and footnotes at 3.29 : 1.

## 5. Shortest path to 70 or more

Current 63 (28.5 / 45). 70 needs 31.5 / 45, so +3 lens points. In order of value:

1. **One truth when tracking is lost.** When the table is lost, lost-and-re-finding, or fewer than half the expected balls are seen: replace the capsule text (never show a tick or "Tracking N" at the same time), give the capsule an amber fill (#FFC247 at 20 % over the dark capsule, plus a 2 pt amber stroke), hide the shot card, and show no separate "Lost the table" pill. Show the next step in the capsule: "Lost the table - hold still" then "Table moved? Tap to set up again" with the capsule itself a button, and put "Set up table" back in the bar in that state. Raises i and a. [M] for the overlay fade.
2. **Make "On line" true.** Show "On line" (green) only when a pocket is called and the aim is within tolerance of that pocket's line; off line show "Off line - aim left" or "aim right" in amber; with no pocket called show nothing in that slot. Add the called pocket to the card as its own line: "CALLED: FAR-RIGHT CORNER" at 22 pt or larger. Raises i and e.
3. **Name balls by number first.** Headline "7 MAROON -> LEFT SIDE", "15 MAROON STRIPE -> ...", "8-BALL -> ..." at the current 52 px cap. Take the name from the same track that draws the highlight, so card and overlay cannot disagree. Raises i and e.
4. **Make the object leg the strongest mark.** Object leg at least 14 px with a light green such as #5BE99F (about 8 : 1 on this cloth by calculation from the sampled cloth luminance), cue leg dropped to a 10 px white at 70 %, and the pocket ring in a distinct hue (white ring, 3 px) centred on the mouth at 1.5 x the mouth width. Needs a device still with the pocket mouth marked to verify the end point. Raises c.
5. **Status at 28 pt, payoff at 48 pt.** Capsule, bar state line, button labels and pills to 28 pt (cap about 39 px = 3.8 mm = 6.5 arcmin at 2 m). A make takes the card slot for 1.2 s: "MADE IT" at 48 pt in green, then "+1" and "3 IN A ROW" at 34 pt, then the card returns; misses and scratches use the same slot at 34 pt in amber or salmon, so nothing stacks under the card. Raises h and d. [M] for timing, sound and haptics.
6. **Settings in player words.** "Device is parked" to "iPad on a stand"; move "Visible-miss grace" to developer mode; "Guide speed" to "Shot strength: soft / medium / firm"; drop "path budget on ricochets" and "snapped to your remembered table". Footnotes to #6C6C70 or darker (about 4.6 : 1 on #F2F2F7). Raises f and g.
7. **HARD is not an error.** Give HARD its own colour (for example orange #FF9F43 with the triangle) and keep red only for BLOCKED; make the guide subtitle white, not tier-coloured, and say "Hit right of centre". Raises b and e a little.

Estimated effect: items 1 to 3 give about +1.5 (i 2.5 to 3.5, e 3.5 to 4), item 4 about +0.5 (c), item 5 about +1 (h, d), item 6 about +0.75 (f, g). That is about 32.3 / 45 = 72. Items 1 and 2 are the cheapest points: they are correctness fixes, not new design.

Cannot be judged from stills: the fade in item 1, the timing, sound and haptics in item 5, and anything about line draw-in or pulses.

## 7. What I could not judge

- The AR calibration overlay: corner handles, Lock and Cancel, "Use pockets", pocket-sighting prompts (not reachable in the Simulator; no device still).
- Any bright room, glare or daylight scene; all device stills are one dim room.
- Overlay in called-shot mode, the called-pocket marker, the post-contact path, and a FAIR or HARD shot on the real table.
- Whether the "Table moved? Re-calibrate" capsule is tappable.
- Cue-ball guide words other than Draw and Stun (per `state-gallery.md` the app does not produce them).
- Tier thresholds (cut angle per tier); one device still gives only an estimate.
- Everything motion: line draw-in, pill entry and exit, the overlay fade on loss, payoff timing, haptics and sound. Reduce Motion and left-handed layout.
- Real-device rendering of the current gallery build's HUD (the newest device still is one build older; layouts match).
